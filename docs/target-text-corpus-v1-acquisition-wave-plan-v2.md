# V1-Core acquisition wave plan V2

This offline plan converts the verified capacity gap into four source-specific
residual targets while keeping every acquisition gate closed.

- Confirmed regulator-linked: 60 development + 15 holdout.
- Confirmed CFTC RED-linked: 60 + 15; blocked until a documented lawful
  capacity route exists.
- Legitimate IAPD-linked: 60 + 15.
- Legitimate SEC EDGAR-linked: 27 + 6, conditional on the existing 42
  candidates later passing complete admission QA.

Development and external holdout have distinct deterministic namespaces.
Opened or owner-accepted hosts cannot be reused, cross-channel overlap is
forbidden, and neither manual cherry-picking nor model ranking is allowed.

The language overlay still requires 24 non-English development groups,
including 12 Vietnamese, and 6 non-English holdout groups, including 4
Vietnamese. Only human review of captured visible text may provide language
credit.

This plan authorizes no enumeration or network request. Each ready channel
requires its own hash-bound enumeration release; CFTC remains blocked rather
than being silently replaced or subsidized by another channel.
