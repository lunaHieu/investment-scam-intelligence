## 1. Financial Markets Authority (New Zealand) — direct copy or export for two listed entities

Official routes:

- General contact: https://www.fma.govt.nz/contact/
- Official Information Act information: https://www.fma.govt.nz/contact/official-information-act-requests/
- Relevant public list: https://www.fma.govt.nz/library/warnings-and-alerts/entities-falsely-claiming-nz-registration

The FMA contact page provides an “Ask a question” route. Its OIA page says the Act gives New Zealanders a right to request official information, requests should specify the information, responses are generally due within 20 working days, and costs may apply. Do not assume the project owner is eligible for an OIA request; first use the general enquiry route to ask for an accessible public copy/export or the correct process.

### Draft inquiry (English)

Subject: Request for an accessible public copy/export of an FMA warning list

Hello,

I am conducting a non-commercial research project on investment-related deceptive content. I am trying to verify two entries shown in FMA search results for “Entities falsely claiming New Zealand registration” (Benefittrade / benefittrade.org and Banktrustcoin / banktrustcoin.com; both shown with the date 30 October 2025).

The list page is not currently rendering its entry content through my available access route. Could you please point me to an accessible official copy, downloadable export, or alternative official page for those two entries? If there are terms governing retention or academic use, please share the relevant terms or advise whether retaining only the citation and limited entity/domain metadata for internal academic research is permitted.

I am not requesting complaint files, personal information, non-public investigative material, or a conclusion about any specific message. I will cite the official source and preserve the distinction between an FMA warning and a ground-truth label.

If a formal information request is required, please advise whether a researcher based outside New Zealand may use that route and whether any fee or eligibility rule applies.

Thank you.

### Scope guard

Use the general enquiry route first. Do not file an OIA request until eligibility and cost implications are clear. Do not retry the previously rate-limited list URL or use access-control workarounds.
